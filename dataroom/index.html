Hydrodomus Data Room

Hydrodomus is building an at-home hydrogen fueling appliance for fuel-cell vehicles. This page consolidates the materials shared with prospective investors during due diligence. Its go-to-market is not selling the device to automakers directly. The goal is for OEMs to list Hydrodomus as an at-home fueling option alongside the vehicle on their own configurator/website, while Hydrodomus retains pricing control over the unit itself.
